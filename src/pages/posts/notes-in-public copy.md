You do not need to be an expert to write something useful. A clear note about what you tried, what surprised you, and what you would do differently can help someone who is a few steps behind.

## Leave a trail

When I learn something new, I try to keep the useful bits close by: the question I started with, the approach I tested, and the detail I do not want to forget next week.

Over time those notes become more than a record. They help reveal patterns, make it easier to return to old projects, and give other people a place to join the conversation.

The best notes are honest about what is still uncertain. A small, specific observation is enough to begin.
