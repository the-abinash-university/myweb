Most projects begin with a question, a rough sketch, or a tiny annoyance that might be worth fixing. The trick is not to turn that first spark into a grand plan too quickly.

## Make the first version small

Choose one useful thing the project should do, then make that part work from beginning to end. A small, complete first version is easier to learn from than a long list of unfinished features.

Some questions I like to ask at the start:

- Who is this for?
- What is the one thing it should help them do?
- What is the smallest version I could share?

The answers can change as the idea takes shape. Getting something simple in front of people often teaches more than polishing an idea in private.
