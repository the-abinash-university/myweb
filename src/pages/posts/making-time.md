Side projects are supposed to make room for curiosity, but it is easy to turn them into another source of pressure. A little structure helps keep the fun part in view.

Instead of waiting for a free weekend, try setting a small finish line for one session: sketch a screen, write a paragraph, or get one interaction working. A visible next step makes it easier to pick the project up again.

It also helps to keep a short list of ideas that are deliberately out of scope. You can come back to them later without letting them distract from the thing you chose to make today.

Progress can be small and still count. The point is to keep following the curiosity that started the project in the first place.
