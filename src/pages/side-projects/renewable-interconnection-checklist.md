> **Sample concept:** This is a planning aid, not an interconnection approval, grid-impact study, or claim of project experience.

## The question

What information should be gathered before beginning a preliminary review of a proposed solar or storage connection?

## A possible approach

Organize the intake around the point of interconnection, proposed technology, nameplate ratings, inverter documentation, expected operating modes, and available network information. Make missing inputs and applicable utility requirements explicit rather than guessing them.

## Example checklist sections

- Project location, point of connection, and requested capacity.
- Inverter, protection, and control documentation.
- Operating scenarios, reactive-power capability, and export limits.
- Required utility studies, data gaps, and review milestones.

Requirements vary by jurisdiction and utility. Confirm the current interconnection process before using a checklist on a real project.
